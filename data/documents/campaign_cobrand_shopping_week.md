# Campaign brief: Co-brand Shopping Week (observational)

Property: IR01  
Window: March (year 2 of synthetic calendar)  
Partner: Brand Boutique A (leased retail)

## Intent
Drive member footfall to the co-brand partner via voucher offers and contact list outreach.

## Measurement available to IR
- Campaign contacts (member_id, contact date)
- IR-issued voucher issue/redeem events
- Tenant-reported **redemption counts** (no ticket GMV)

## Explicit non-claims
This brief is observational. It does **not** establish that the campaign caused incremental
mall revenue or tenant GMV. No randomized assignment was used.
